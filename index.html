FleetBook

Trips, profit & loss, and who owes whom, for your truck, van or car.

FleetBook is a simple bookkeeping app for owners of one or two vehicles. Record every trip and expense, see what each vehicle really earns, and keep track of what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers. Your records stay on your phone and are backed up to your own Google Drive.

Coming soon to Google Play

For Android phones.

What FleetBook does

Trips

Record the vehicle, date, route, customer, freight and advance received. Each trip shows its own profit after fuel, tolls and other trip costs.

Expenses

Fuel, repairs, tyres, driver pay, token tax, insurance and loan installments, paid in cash or on credit.

Receivable & payable

A ledger for every customer, fuel station, workshop and driver. See at a glance who owes you and whom you owe.

Profit & loss

Income, expenses by category and net profit by month or year, for all vehicles or one vehicle.

Backup & restore

Automatic daily or weekly backup to your Google Drive. On a new phone, sign in and get everything back.

Share statements

Send a party's statement or a profit & loss summary on WhatsApp or any other app.

Why FleetBook asks for your Google account

FleetBook has no servers. It uses your Google account so that your backups are stored in your own Google Drive, where only you and FleetBook can reach them.

  • Sign in with Google tells FleetBook which account your backups belong to. FleetBook keeps only your name and email address, on your phone.
  • Google Drive access is limited to a hidden folder that only FleetBook can open (Google describes it as "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive", drive.appdata). FleetBook cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your records go straight from your phone to your Drive. We never receive them, and FleetBook has no ads and no analytics.
  • You can delete your backups and remove FleetBook's access at any time.
